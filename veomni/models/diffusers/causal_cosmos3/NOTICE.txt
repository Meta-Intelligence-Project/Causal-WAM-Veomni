integration use VeOmni tensor/layout interfaces and do not import Cosmos runtime.

Optional decision-window policy training references the finite clean-history,
current-state and future-only supervision contract in NVlabs/LongLive Long-WAM
at 242635807b4ebb89fa2609a84df7eacde6aed92f (Apache-2.0). Native Cosmos packing,
flow matching, shared diffusion time and model architecture are retained; no
Long-WAM model weights or source implementation are included by this feature.

Shared MoT, Nemotron/Qwen layer mathematics, causal action visibility and
grouped TND backward follow the referenced source.

TrainTimeWeight and compute_flow_matching_loss from the same commit. Random and

draw order and the loss calculation body are preserved.
